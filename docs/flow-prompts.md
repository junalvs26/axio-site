# Prompts Google Flow — Site Axio

Regras para todas as cenas (cole no início de cada prompt ou use como estilo fixo):

> STYLE: photorealistic cinematic sci-fi, premium automotive commercial look, anamorphic lens,
> shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, volumetric light, night, wet reflective surfaces.
> Palette: deep navy #001A3E and near-black dominate; the ONLY warm color is a precise orange
> #FF6B23 light. Clean, controlled, elegant. No war-zone, no rubble field, no explosions, no
> neon RGB, no cyberpunk clutter, no cartoon, no anime, no visible text.
> ROBOT: original design, elite combat unit, ~4 m tall, upright athletic humanoid proportions
> (broad shoulders, narrow waist, long legs) — NOT hunched, NOT gorilla-like. Armor is smooth,
> seamless matte navy-black ceramic plates (#001A3E undertone) with precise thin panel lines,
> like a luxury sports car or a stealth aircraft. No exposed cables, no hoses, no scratches,
> no rust, no clutter. Very thin orange #FF6B23 light lines run along the panel seams of the
> chest, forearms and calves, glowing softly. Head: compact, sleek helmet with a single
> horizontal capsule-shaped visor (a long rounded rectangle) glowing orange, and one small round
> orange sensor dot above the right edge of the visor. Subtle emblem: a minimal engraved
> chevron "Λ" with a small dot beneath it, debossed on the left shoulder plate, catching light
> only at an angle — tone-on-tone, barely visible. Feels powerful, precise, silent, inevitable.
> Advanced, premium, combat-ready — never toy-like, never a mascot.

Gere primeiro uma IMAGEM de referência do robô em pé, 3/4 de frente, fundo escuro neutro, e use
essa mesma imagem como referência (ingredient) em todas as cenas para manter consistência.
Duração sugerida: 6–8 s. Gerar 16:9 (desktop) e 9:16 (mobile) de cada.

### Prompt da imagem de referência
Full-body studio portrait of the ROBOT standing still, three-quarter front view, arms relaxed,
dark navy seamless background, single soft key light from the upper left and a thin orange rim
light, orange visor and seam lines glowing, emblem on the left shoulder subtly visible.
Hyper-detailed, clean, product-photography quality.

---

## 01 · impact (a chegada — uma única tomada contínua)
Obs.: tudo em câmera lenta cinematográfica contínua — o scroll do site controla a velocidade.

Night, wet empty city plaza, rain falling, near-total darkness. Camera very low, close to the
wet ground, looking slightly up. A thin orange streak dives out of the dark sky — fast, controlled,
with intent. The robot lands feet-first in a powerful superhero landing in the center of frame:
one knee down, one fist striking the ground, other arm back, head lowered. On contact, a heavy
circular shockwave blasts outward across the wet ground: puddles burst into sheets of water,
the pavement cracks radially beneath the fist with a faint orange glow inside the cracks, and
chunks of debris, stones and concrete fragments are thrown UP into the air around the robot,
rising and hanging in slow motion with rain droplets, lit by orange reflections. The camera
takes one heavy jolt from the impact. While the debris is still floating upward, the robot
slowly raises its head and lifts its gaze toward the camera. The camera pushes in smoothly and
racks focus onto its capsule visor, which ignites bright orange #FF6B23 from dark, flickers once
and locks on; the orange light rakes across the smooth matte helmet; the small round sensor
above the visor lights a beat later. Debris begins to fall back down around it.
Last frame: close-up of the robot's face, glowing orange visor centered and staring into the
lens, debris and rain suspended around, dark negative space above.

## 02 · awakening (o olhar — continuação direta da 01)
Start exactly on the close-up of the glowing orange visor staring into the lens, debris and rain
suspended in the air. Extreme close-up, very slow push-in. The visor optics make a precise
focusing adjustment, like a lens locking onto a target; the orange glow intensifies and pulses
once, lighting the falling raindrops in front of it. Hold, intense and still.
Then the camera begins a slow pull back, revealing the helmet and shoulders, the subtle engraved
emblem on the left shoulder catching the orange light for a moment.
Last frame: robot head and shoulders, visor glowing, dark negative space above for a title.

## 03 · city
Continue pulling back and crane up behind the robot, still kneeling in its landing pose, to reveal a vast rainy city at night: towers,
distant lights, traffic streaks, humid haze. Rack focus to a glass office high in a nearby
building: a realistic businessperson (30s–40s, tired, sleeves rolled up) alone at a desk, lit by
multiple screens and a phone constantly lighting up with notifications, hand on forehead.
Then cut back: the robot slowly turns its head toward that window; visor optics adjust.
Last frame: robot profile looking at the lit window.

## 04 · analysis
From the robot's visor, a precise holographic projection in thin orange and pale-blue lines
spreads over the city: fine data points, connection lines between buildings, scattered fragments
flickering and disconnected. Camera pushes through the hologram layers, passing data nodes.
Elegant, minimal, readable negative space — not cluttered. Last frame: the hologram converging
into a single bright orange point.

## 05 · action
The robot rises from the landing pose — slow, heavy, deliberate; armor plates shift with
precision, orange seam lines brighten as it stands, water sheets off the armor. It begins to walk through the rainy street toward the building.
Tracking shot low at ground level following its feet: each footstep hits a puddle with weight
and a small shockwave. (Gerar 4 variações de ângulo: lateral tracking, low front, over-the-shoulder,
wide with city — uma para cada passo/capítulo.) Last frame: robot reaching the base of the tower.

## 06 · solution
Macro, almost abstract: camera enters the robot's chest through a gap in the armor. Inside,
precise machinery — cables, processing cores, heat sinks. Orange energy pulses travel along cables
and converge into a central core that brightens. Dark navy interior, orange light only on the
flowing energy. Camera travels through and exits. Last frame: soft orange glow filling frame.

## 07 · transformation
Wide shot of the same city, still night, but warmer: more windows lit, rain easing to mist.
The businessperson walks confidently out of the building onto the street, phone in pocket,
looking ahead. The robot stands beside and slightly behind them, still and protective, like
infrastructure. Slow lateral dolly. Last frame: both figures, the robot towering, calm.

## 09 · final
The robot standing on an elevated platform at the brightest point of the city skyline, wind
and mist. It turns its head and looks directly into the lens. Slow push-in toward the visor,
which brightens orange intensely. Last frame: visor filling the center, dark around it for the
call to action.
